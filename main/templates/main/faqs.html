Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some quick answers to common questions about using the CraftyKids platform.

What is CraftyKids?

CraftyKids is a learning platform where students can explore videos and flipbooks with fun DIY and educational activities.

Who can use this platform?

Students, teachers, parents, and school staff can use CraftyKids to support classroom and at-home learning.

Do I need to pay to use CraftyKids?

No. The platform is designed as a school learning resource for children and their families.

What can I find here?

You can find educational videos, flipbooks, and creative activity ideas that help make learning more interactive and enjoyable.

Who do I contact if I need help?

You may contact Progressive Elementary School through 136597@deped.gov.ph or (046) 434 0253 for support and inquiries.
