Our Purpose

CraftyKids is designed to help elementary students learn through creative, hands-on DIY projects. We believe that combining structured education with fun, colorful activities helps children retain knowledge better while developing their fine motor skills and creativity.

🏫

Project Background

This platform was specially developed for Progressive Elementary School. Our goal is to provide a safe, engaging online environment where students, teachers, and parents can collaborate and access high-quality educational materials anytime, anywhere.
